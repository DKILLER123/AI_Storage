Dramatis Personae

Characters

A few faces of the Seoul this book lives in. Portraits matched to the roles, with real-name details where a character has a real-life counterpart. New faces are added each installment.

Baek Si-on

The protagonist. Former idol, freshly-discharged soldier.

NameBaek Si-on (백시온)
Age22
BackgroundKorean-American
FormerIdol · A’ST1
CurrentActor (soon)
StatusReincarnated

The Debt Collector Who Was Once a Star

Raised between Seoul and Los Angeles, Baek Si-on was pushed onto the stage by a father who believed the Hallyu wave would carry his son to the top. It didn’t. The group fizzled, the lights went out, and he spent two years in the Korean military. Then he woke up somewhere else — carrying the name, and the memory, of a life he never lived.

Now he’s a debt collector for a week and an actor by instinct, the kind of person who reads a screenplay and rewrites it in his head before the director can blink.
