Character Introductions

Character Intros

Every face introduced into the story, gathered in one place. Tap a name to open a portrait — the page stays put and nothing else moves. The left-hand list on the Contents page lists these in the order they first appear in the book.

Baek Si-on

The protagonist · Debt collector, then actor

Korean-American · 22 · reincarnated

Raised between Seoul and Los Angeles, pushed onto the stage by a father who believed the Hallyu wave would carry his son to the top. He spent two years in the Korean military, then woke up carrying the name — and the memory — of a life he never lived.

Yoon Hye-ja

Si-on's mother

Mid-40s · steel behind a calm face

Short hair, hard eyes, and a way of putting two billion won on the table because she doesn't want her son eating meatless lunchboxes on set.

Baek Jeong-hun

Uncle & indie film director

37 · Green Bottle Fly

Too thin, too tired, a pencil behind his ear and a screenplay six years in the making. With a nephew he barely knows, he finally climbs the stairs of his brother's house.

Lee Ji-eun (IU)

Singer-songwriter · Actor · LOEN Entertainment

The Nation's Little Sister · real-name counterpart

The one the whole country calls its little sister. When a fan letter about a half-basement and a debt collector reaches her hands, she does the one thing her manager warns her against: she decides to help.

Kim Se-jeong

The debtor's daughter, dreaming of the stage

She works the grill · idolises IU

She counts the hours and hides a letter to her favourite singer under her mattress. The worst thing in the world and the best thing in the world can be the same person.

Bae Jong-han

Team leader · LOEN Entertainment

Manager · the quiet negotiator behind the scenes

The one who asks the sensible questions and nods slowly. He knows exactly what's at stake the moment a star decides to help a stranger, and he keeps the ledger straight when everyone else is busy feeling things.

Jeong Han-teuk

Artist management · LOEN Entertainment

Agency assistant · the one who came to verify

The man dispatched to confirm a fan letter's plea — the first of LOEN's people to meet the debt collector face to face. Mild-mannered, easily rattled, and terrified of being folded into a chokehold twice in one day.

Choi Jin-ri (Sulli)

Singer · Actor · formerly of f(x)

The beautiful twenty-year-old at the coldest point of her storm

One of the most beautiful twenty-year-olds in the whole country, and the one Baek Si-on already knows the ending for. She says her name is Choi Jin-ri, not Choi Sulli.

Baek Eun-ah

Si-on's cousin · SM trainee

Still carrying the smell of the dance practice room

A seventeen-year-old trainee who still has dance sweat on her, and the bridge between her uncle's film and the people who live inside it.

Kim Hyo-yeon

Singer · Dancer · Girls' Generation

Born Kim Hyo-yeon · on stage, Hyoyeon

Girls' Generation's main dancer and one of SM's older seniors — cheerful, relentless, and the one who would drag Jin-ri out toward the industry's nightlife.

Kim Jae-kyung

Singer · Dancer · Leader of Rainbow

Rainbow's leader, who still makes things by hand

A former JYP trainee who never stopped being a craftsperson. The leather bags she turns out carry no logo, only her own two hands.

Goo Hara

Singer · Dancer · KARA

KARA's visual, kind even under fire

Born 1991 in Gwangju. The one who, while her own group stood blockaded by fans, still had the presence of mind to hand a near-stranger a name and a number.

Krystal Jung

Singer · Actor · f(x)

The youngest blade of SM's seventh floor

Stage name Krystal; registered name Jung Soo-jung. f(x)'s youngest — a born performer who had just had a taste of being more than "the group's youngest," and couldn't put it back down.

Song Qian (Victoria)

Singer · Dancer · Leader of f(x)

Born Song Qian in Qingdao · on stage, Victoria

The first Chinese leader of an SM girl group. f(x)'s anchor — the one the practice room quietly leans on, and the one pressing record when the others have stopped.

Jung Jae-joon

Independent producer · studio 401

The ear that finds the note

An underground producer working out of a cluttered fourth-floor studio. To Baek Si-on he is less a business partner than a translator, reading a picture in someone's head and writing it in sound.

Son Nam-won

Editor-in-chief of OSEN's entertainment desk

The man who lives by the scoop

The kind of editor who dug with his mouth instead of his feet. Publicly bruised by Dispatch and afraid of losing a second exclusive, he is exactly the audience a half-exclusive was built for.

Park Ji-hun

Stylist · JB Hair, Hapjeong-dong

Three years of artist styling at a Cheongdam-dong salon

The front-desk stylist who chased Baek Si-on out of the shop to offer himself. Three years in artist styling, a portfolio ready, and the good sense to read a million-won membership card for the door it was.

Irene

Leader of Red Velvet

Real-name counterpart · Bae Joo-hyun

The calm at the centre of a debuting group, the one who calls "one, two, three" and makes four voices land on the same syllable. On the day Red Velvet debuted she sized up Baek Si-on's name on the sheet of A4, shut the nostalgia gate in her own head, and led the four of them through the bow.

Kang Seul-gi

Red Velvet

Real-name counterpart · Kang Seul-gi

The member who leans half her body out from behind the leader's shoulder to ask the question nobody else will — bright, unguarded, and the first to let her curiosity slip out before the politeness catches up.

Son Seung-wan

Red Velvet

Real-name counterpart · Son Seung-wan

The kind of face that makes a room decide to be friendly; the member whose warmth reads even in a crowd of strangers, and the one who makes it okay to be nervous out loud.

Park Soo-young

Red Velvet · maknae

Real-name counterpart · Park Soo-young

The maknae, the one who leans slightly toward Irene in the dark of a broadcast hall to ask whether Baek sunbae can really take number one. Her optimism comes in its youngest, least armoured form.

Alexandra Daddario

American actress · main competition, Venice

Real-name counterpart · Alexandra Daddario

The woman who sits down across from the man eating alone in the dark and tells him he is either the least interested or the hungriest person on the beach. Direct in a way Seoul's politeness never is — and the first on that beach not to come away with a phone number.

Luisa Ranieri

Italian actress · host & red carpet, Venice

Real-name counterpart · Luisa Ranieri

The face of the Venice red carpet — the Italian actress who draws the flashbulbs and opens the night. Warm, unhurried, and photographed against the Lido's sea-light; the woman who makes the premiere feel less like a competition and more like a night out.

Tim Roth

British actor · president of the main competition, Venice

Real-name counterpart · Tim Roth

The jury president — an English actor with a name known to every filmmaker in the room, and a gaze that weighs a film in a single pass. Where he sits, the whole conversation between the jury and the press pivots.

Alexandre Desplat

French composer · jury, Venice

Real-name counterpart · Alexandre Desplat

One of the great living film composers — the man whose name on a poster already promises a certain sound. On the jury he is the quiet one who listens for the score beneath the picture.

Sandy Powell

British costume designer · jury, Venice

Real-name counterpart · Sandy Powell

A costume designer with an Oscar shelf, and a sharp eye for what a single seam can do. The woman who will notice the gown before anyone else notices the wearer — and the voice that settles whether a red carpet is talk or is art.

Jessica Hausner

Austrian director · jury, Venice

Real-name counterpart · Jessica Hausner

An Austrian director of precise, unsettling films. On the jury she is the one who looks at a picture the way a surgeon looks at a cut — calmly, and far too closely.

Philip Gröning

German director · jury, Venice

Real-name counterpart · Philip Gröning

A German documentarian of slow, patient films — the sort who can hold a shot long enough to watch a person change their mind. He brings the patience of the monastery to the prize table.

Elia Suleiman

Palestinian director · jury, Venice

Real-name counterpart · Elia Suleiman

A filmmaker of deadpan comic stillness, forever watching borders and the small absurdities in between. On the jury he is the one who laughs at the wrong moment — and is usually right to.

Park Jae-won

Reporter · Insight

OC · early twenties · one of three full-time staff

The junior who still asks where a number came from, and who will, when the time comes, tie a three-thousand-won badge to his forehead in a scrum of thirty cameras. Earnest. Slightly too short for the steel forest of ENG lenses. Not a fan — his older sister is.

Kang Soo-hyuk

Entertainment reporter · Dispatch

OC · night shift · the man who lost a news war by ten seconds

A dirt-digger by trade who, on this particular night, found a drop of patriotic heat still burning under the gossip. He would have had the flash. He did not. Someone else had written the ending before the speech was over.

Roy Andersson

Director · Golden Lion, 71st Venice

Real-name counterpart · Sweden

A living fossil of European art cinema, and the man who would, later that night, walk over with a glass of red and tell a twenty-two-year-old that the young man should drink. On the official photograph he only turned his head and said, You are very young.

Franca Sozzani

Editor-in-chief · Vogue Italia

Real-name counterpart · Italy · more than twenty years at the top of the chain

A woman in her sixties with a signature fall of blonde waves. Editors-in-chief do not appear at every sitting. A Volpi Cup Best Actor sitting is not every sitting. The card she put in his hand was not courtesy. It was a judgment: worth investing in.

Kim Hae-yeon

Baek Jeong-hun’s wife · Baek Eun-ah’s mother

OC · the one who kept the house while Venice kept the husband

A woman in her late forties with a face that had learned to be kind without becoming soft. She is the reason the pork is in the pan, and the reason a presidential telegram waits for the pears.

Kim Go-eun

Actress · K-Arts acting

Real-name counterpart · the aisle seat in the third row

A face the industry had already started to use, sitting in a student seat as if the industry had not. She takes the dishcloth home, and credits the mother.

Park So-dam

Actress · K-Arts

Real-name counterpart · a Veteran cameo still wet on the résumé

She wanted the eight minutes. She takes the 11 p.m. hunger instead. It is not cheaper. It just does not come with a lion.

Dean Choi

Dean · School of Film, TV and Multimedia · K-Arts

OC · tortoise-shell glasses · a vanity that did its own typing

He invites a trophy into a school and would like the school to remember who opened the door. He speaks in complete sentences. The students have learned to wait for the full stop.

Seo Eun-ju

Counsel · Kim & Chang · entertainment contracts

OC · she doesn’t do song splits · she does the other kind of money

Mid-thirties, a navy blazer, an ivory blouse, a chain so fine it only caught when she turned her head. Jeong Han-teuk sent the card. She does not do first meetings in offices. She does them over soft tofu, where a man has to put his phone down to use a spoon.

Kobe Bryant

Guard · Los Angeles Lakers · five-time NBA champion

Real-name counterpart · the Mamba on a Saturday morning in L.A.

The face the world already had a word for, filling a laptop in Hapjeong because he had asked who the Korean was, and because Rich Paul does not send an e-mail when that question has been asked. Show up, or don’t.

Park Chan-wook

Director · Joint Security Area, Oldboy, The Handmaiden (in the drawer)

Real-name counterpart · DGK, unofficially, over charcoal

Navy, glasses, a mouth that has already decided whether a close-up is a kindness or a knife. He does not waste a greeting. He spends it.

Bong Joon-ho

Director · Memories of Murder, The Host, Snowpiercer

Real-name counterpart · the man the table calls Bong-suk

He stands in a doorway as if doorways are where stories decide whether to come in. The hair does the talking until the mouth is ready. When it is ready, it is kind.

Song Kang-ho

Actor · Memories of Murder, The Host, The Attorney

Real-name counterpart · the smoke he had been promised

The smile is not a performance. It is a decision to be in the room. He calls Bong Joon-ho Bong-suk the way a man calls a younger brother a name that will outlive both of them.

Ryan Mitchell

Brand advisor · Hyundai Motor · Detroit, then wherever the car points

OC · the American who takes zero notes

Early forties, silver glasses, a suit that does not ask what city it is in. He wins the arguments that cost money. If it is worth keeping, he will remember it. If he does not remember it, it was not worth the ink.

Lee Joon-ik

Director · King and the Clown, Sado

Real-name counterpart · Buam, a kettle, a prince who has not yet been cast as a yes

Shaved head, round glasses, a mouth that smiles as if smiling were a way of not wasting a morning. He pours the tea first. The tea is the first scene.

Scooter Braun

Manager · SB Projects · the rooms that do not send e-mail when the question is a person

Real-name counterpart · West Hollywood, a table, twenty percent that is a map

He smiles as if smiling were a first clause. Justin. Ariana. A list that is not the point. The point is a week if you have a week, and a territory a cousin in Seoul cannot cover from a gate.

Justin Bieber

Singer · SB Projects · the man who still tweets at midnight

Real-name counterpart · Matsuhisa, a blonde head, a tweet that is a pipe

He eats like a boy who has been famous too long to remember being hungry. He tells a Best Actor to drop a song and then, three days later, does the work of telling a continent. Controversy and traffic have never been opposites.

Ariana Grande

Singer · SB Projects · a high note that arrives before the introduction is finished

Real-name counterpart · Matsuhisa, a ponytail, a nickname she hands over herself

She sits down as if sitting were a decision she had already made. She tells a Korean Best Actor to call her Ari. She does not ask whether the table is a table she is allowed to occupy.

Taylor Swift

Singer · the first-ever Dick Clark Award of Achievement

Real-name counterpart · a number written on an apple, in ink, in a room full of cameras

She kisses cheeks the way other people sign autographs — warmly, publicly, and with consequences that echo on three charts at once. She loans a silk square and receives it back carrying tears and an entire news cycle. She calls it diplomacy. The charts call it an encirclement.

Kim Nam-joon

Rapper · Big Hit · Bangtan Sonyeondan · RM

Real-name counterpart · a sunset stage, a boy who knows the difference between a collab and a charity

He hears a Best Actor say we, not you, and the word lands like a door that had been closed. He does not bow as if the bow were the whole of the work. He asks whether the man in front of him is joking, because he has been trained to assume that men like that are joking.

Kim Tae-hyung

Vocal · Big Hit · Bangtan Sonyeondan · V

Real-name counterpart · a boxy smile, a boy who says hyung to a Best Actor as if hyung were weather

He is not the one who asks. He is the one who arrives already decided that the man in front of him is a hyung. The smile is a box. The box is not a performance. It is how his face is built.

Kim Seok-jin

Vocal · Big Hit · Bangtan Sonyeondan · Jin

Real-name counterpart · a joke that arrives before fear has finished sitting down

He is the oldest. He uses it. He tells a Best Actor that the oldest has to look after the youngest, and he means the sentence as a joke and as a job. The face is a face magazines will later argue about. Tonight it is a boy on a sunset stage who has decided not to be frightened in public.

Park Ji-min

Vocal · Big Hit · Bangtan Sonyeondan · Jimin

Real-name counterpart · a small body that occupies more stage than the body should

He is listed on a sunset flyer under a name the West has not yet learned to say. He does not speak first. He does not need to. The body is the sentence.

Jeon Jung-kook

Vocal · Big Hit · Bangtan Sonyeondan · Jungkook

Real-name counterpart · the maknae, a stare that has not yet learned to hide

He is the youngest. The flyer says so in a font that does not care. He watches a Best Actor the way a boy watches a door that has just opened in a wall he thought was solid.

Min Yoon-gi

Rapper · producer · Big Hit · Bangtan Sonyeondan · SUGA

Real-name counterpart · a mouth that does not waste a line, a beat that does not wait to be invited

He is the one who will later make a studio feel like a room that has opinions. Tonight he is a name on a flyer and a boy who does not clap because clapping is a sentence he has not decided to say.

Jung Ho-seok

Rapper · dancer · Big Hit · Bangtan Sonyeondan · J-Hope

Real-name counterpart · a smile that does the work of a light, a body that does the work of a drum

He is the one the audience finds with the eyes first. Hope, on a flyer, looks like a slogan. On a stage it looks like a boy who has decided the sunset is a cue, not a mood.

Bang Si-hyuk

Founder · Big Hit Entertainment · the man the industry will later call Bang PD

Real-name counterpart · round glasses, a swivel chair, chicken that has just turned sweet

He eats when he cannot do anything else. He takes unknown calls because not taking them is a luxury he does not have. Talent is a word he has not heard in a month. He will sign without a ratchet, because a drowning man does not negotiate the thickness of the rope.

James Corden

Host · The Late Late Show · CBS

Real-name counterpart · a Range Rover, four cameras, a hug that lands harder than it looks

He hears Legend in rehearsal and immediately wants a car. He sings harmony in a body that should not be able to, and then howls a chorus until the note breaks and does not stop. He tells a Best Actor to smile more. He means it as a note, not a kindness.

Kim Min-soo

Part-timer · Kyobo Book Centre · Kyung Hee, on leave

OC · twenty-four, two jobs, not a fan, not not a fan

Official reason for the leave: personal. Actual reason: tuition. Seven-thirty at the shop, three at the chicken place in Hongdae. He knows what Vogue is. He also knows he is not the person it is for. He is the person who opens the box. He buys one anyway, at staff price, to keep.

Yoon Hyun-sang

Singer · LOEN · the junior at the piano

OC · a tunnel set, an upright, a lift he did not ask to be explained

He has a face, a throat, and hands that know a keyboard. In this city’s new-artist pond that is not yet a name. He sits where the director pointed.

Daniel

Show caller · Riot Games · Season 4 Worlds

OC · a rundown, a headset, a stadium that is still empty

He wants a war-drum into a rock chorus without a seam. He wants bodies in the wide shot of a hook. He does not need the singer to dance.

Hwang Soo-ah

Director · Love Yourself MV

OC · nail holes, a MacBook, a snap when two people start a fire too early

She books apartments that have been lived in. She will not unmask an actor too soon. Natural, she says, and means a couple who actually dated.

Yoo In-na

Actress · DJ · MBC Raise the Volume

Real · a honey voice, a chair that has already decided you will talk

Eleven years not debuting as a singer. Then the voice a nation leaves on in the car. She will not let a guest hide behind a prize.

Dan Reynolds

Imagine Dragons · a hole that used to have a name in it

Real · Vegas, a hotel that does not care about Seoul

He looks at a Korean name on a rundown and does not throw a glass. He asks Wayne if the song is actually good.

LeBron James

Small forward · Cleveland Cavaliers

Real · October 2014 · a pool, a camera, a song

He watches four minutes of another man’s legend and feels no envy — only the itch to answer with his body. His agent hands him a challenge and a microphone-stand of a question, and the whole sports world finds out what a hundred and thirteen kilograms can do from a standing start.

Rich Paul

Founder · Klutch Sports · LeBron’s agent

Real · he reads attention the way other people read weather

The man who turns a rival’s spotlight into his client’s headline. One MV, one pool, one well-timed caption — that is the whole of his trade, and he is very good at it.

Jeanie Buss

President · Los Angeles Lakers

Real · Staples Center · the afternoon before the opener

The president who ends a stranger’s rehearsal by walking over to shake his hand, take one photo, and send her assistant after his sister with a business card. Partnership talks, she notes, are worth having later.

James Harden

Guard · Houston Rockets

Real · October 2014 · the visitors’ tunnel

The Beard with two drives and a drawn foul who reminds twenty thousand people that Kobe’s comeback night is not a documentary. He plans the upset from the mouth of the tunnel and does not plan on losing.

Dwight Howard

Centre · Houston Rockets

Real · October 2014 · the elbow at the centre of everything

Elbows out, hands spread, grinning wider the louder the boos get. His defensive rebound starts the fight, and his elbow — swung from two hundred and sixty pounds away — is what nearly ends Baek Si-on’s night.

Adam Levine

Frontman · Maroon 5

Real · courtside, VIP row one

The frontman who calls Baek Si-on’s pre-game show the best he has seen all year, presses a demo into his ear and asks him for a video idea — then spends the rest of the night physically keeping the same singer away from Dwight Howard.

Behati Prinsloo

Victoria’s Secret model · Adam’s wife

Real · the one Adam calls Little Pumpkin

The Victoria’s Secret model who reaches across her husband to press down the same singer’s other arm — and then calls the whole near-death moment “sweet.”

✕ Baek Si-on

Wiz Khalifa

Rapper · the rap half of ‘See You Again’

Pittsburgh · ‘Black and Yellow’ · ‘Blacc Hollywood’

Adam’s friend, the reason an arena playlist runs on ‘Legend’, and a man who keeps the demo Universal threw away saved in his own pocket. He decided Baek Si-on was his brother for life somewhere around three in the morning.

Charlie Puth

YouTube songwriter · the chorus Universal rejected

Two years drifting in Los Angeles · one broken eyebrow · $50,000, refused

He wrote the line for a friend he lost, not for a conference room. Answers the door at 7:30 p.m. with pepper spray in his pocket and prices his dignity at ‘not for sale’ — right up until someone offers five hundred thousand and splits the song from the person. The gap in his eyebrow is the most reliable identification feature in the music industry.

Kim Jae-wook

Entertainment desk chief · a Korean web portal

Iced americano · the qualifier doctrine · “Ship it.”

The editor who refuses to print a bare crown. He bolted Nicki Minaj and Taylor Swift onto a play count, called it serving the nation, and accidentally authored the arithmetic of a country’s mornings.

Lee Mi-kyung

Vice chairwoman · CJ Group

Exile in Los Angeles · the wrong-nephew bet · “You are a very interesting person.”

She helped build an entertainment empire, backed art the wrong minister disliked, and was handed an American “rest period” for it. She sat in the VIP row to price an endorsement and left holding a beverage joint venture, a screenplay aimed at the very top, and a toast poured from a three-dollar sports drink.

Baek Jeong-hoon

Film director · the uncle · Lion of the Future, Venice

Left-leaning stubbornness · immune to olive branches · “He’ll only shoot it more real.”

The family’s award-winning contrarian, allergic to chaebol money since film school. Handed a crime script about a hit list whose No. 1 is a certain teacher, he did not hear a warning. He heard a casting call.

Kevin Hart

Comedian · professional victim of his own height

1.63, officially · self-deprecation as a business model · the booster seat

America’s hardest-working small package. He came for a front-row seat, declined a public execution with impeccable timing, and walked out of the arena as the night’s second headline.

Chang Byung-gyu

Founder-CEO of Bluehole · the investor across the tea table

‘TERA’ · 66 billion won, pre-money · preferred shares, 25%

Built one of the strongest engines in Korean MMORPGs, then watched LOL and mobile eat the market his technology defined. Signs outsourcing “with the feeling of a supreme grandmaster busking on a street corner.” One afternoon, one tea room, and a quarter of his company belongs to a singer.

Yeon Sang-ho

Film director · the previz answer

‘Train to Busan’ · zombie crowd scenes, solved · three rings

Took the call with a set still shouting behind him. Turned a hundred extras and seven zones into a math problem, and pointed the answer toward a struggling game company with “solid fundamentals, rough times.”

Yeri

Red Velvet’s youngest · the quiet one in the sofa corner

Joined not long ago · swallowed laughs · one honest question per evening

Too new to complain loudly. Curled up in the corner while her unnies burned out around her, conducted a sincere compatibility study between “watermelon” and “captain,” and asked the one question everybody else was too polite to ask: this really isn’t an April Fools’ prank, right?

Park Sang-min

Seongsu-dong real-estate agent · fourteen years of shoe factories

A silver scooter · his best jacket · 48 billion won in one afternoon

Spent fourteen years selling aging shoe factories, dead print shops and unwanted warehouses to café-dream budgets — then a masked buyer said “I’ll take it,” he checked his phone, saw the date was April 1, and did the most professional thing of his career: he demanded to be shown where the hidden cameras were.
