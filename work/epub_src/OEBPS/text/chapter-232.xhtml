Baek Si-on finally understood.

The sudden clinginess was the decoy. The old-ledger audit was the core business.

The Choi Jin-ri of a few months ago didn’t operate like this.

The current edition got jealous, snatched phones, and dealt out pickup lines that bordered on reportable offenses.

She also kept books on small debts from months back and invoked them at dawn, with the full authority of the wronged party, to forbid him from leaving.

Truthfully, the willfulness wasn’t a burden.

Still.

Understanding was understanding.

Her face surfaced at the edge of his neck, triumph in her eyes, poorly disguised.

At first, Choi Jin-ri still remembered to warn him about the remaining forty minutes.

Later the warnings came broken, farther and farther apart, until the original purpose of this revenge operation was nowhere in her head.

The credit, as audited
