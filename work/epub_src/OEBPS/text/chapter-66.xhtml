Chapter Sixty-Six

One Promise: Donate It All

August 1 Rehearsal KBS · Music Bank rehearsal floor A conveyor belt, a running order, and the last slot of the day

The rehearsal had turned into a flat-out industrial conveyor belt.

The newcomers were pushed on first.

Blocking. Lip-sync. Hunting the camera's red light.

The music stopped, and before the people on stage could even catch their breath, the director's voice came crashing out of the speakers.

"Next group, get ready."

Cut camera. The lens snapped over to the bank of seats at the side of the stage.

Music Bank's two current fixed MCs were Park Seo-joon and Bora of SISTAR.

The two of them, who'd been resting backstage a moment ago, shifted in an instant into a pair of brimming smiles at the light turning red, and began to trade the pre-memorised linking lines back and forth.

The moment the last syllable dropped.

The lens cut straight back to the main stage, and the next queue of idols, already lined up, launched into their routine on the beat.

Not even a single second of dead air between.

Baek Si-on had been slotted into the closing position by the show team.

That privilege had come at exactly the right moment.

The long money-giving spree earlier had burned off nearly all his waiting time, and this late rehearsal slot happened to hand him back the hours he needed for styling.

A quick sound check, a run-through of the dry vocal, a confirmation of the two main close-up cameras — and his rehearsal was done.

By the time Baek Si-on pushed the door back into single waiting room, there were only fifteen minutes left before the 5 p.m. nationwide live broadcast.

Baek Eun-ah was curled up with her phone on the chair by the makeup counter, screen lit, a KakaoTalk window open.

"Cousin."

"Mm."

"Jin-ri eonni messaged."

"What'd she say?"

"She said she hopes you take number one today. And she sent three cheering emojis."

"Tell her to vote, not just pray."

Baek Eun-ah looked at the three fist emojis Choi Jin-ri had sent on the phone screen, then at her cousin on the couch, eyes closed.

Silently she trimmed Baek Si-on's exact words and typed back: "Cousin says thank you eonni! He'll work hard!"

August 1 5:14 p.m. · live KBS · Music Bank recording hall Warm to cool, and the countdown ticks down

The light banks in the recording hall switched en masse into broadcast mode.

Warm to cool. The LED screens on both sides of the stage lit up in sync with Music Bank's rotating logo, and the countdown numbers ticked frame by frame on the monitors in the control room.

Ten.

Nine.

Eight.

On the wall-mounted TV in the waiting room, the feed switched to the main stage.

Park Seo-joon and Bora stood at centre stage.

Both smiles slotted precisely into place the moment the light went red.

"Hello, everyone! Welcome to KBS Music Bank!"

"Another day full of music and excitement!"

"…"

Baek Si-on leaned against the couch and listened, not watching the TV.

"Eun-ah."

"Mm?"

Baek Eun-ah sat up straight in an instant.

"What's wrong, nervous?"

"No. Tell my mother to cook a little extra tonight."

"…Oh."

Baek Eun-ah pulled out her phone and sent a message to Professor Yoon Hye-ja.

It was only after she'd typed it that it hit her.

She'd eaten breakfast and lunch normally today.

But Baek Si-on, to keep the jawline sharp under the highest-definition lens, had gone all day without a drop of food, washed down only by two iced Americanos in the entire day.

For a man who was famously protective of his food and devoted to the practical, that was a form of physical torture.

No wonder his eyes were shut.

He wasn't resting.

He was enduring.

✿ ✿ ✿

The broadcast went on.

On the wall TV, the newcomers went up group after group, three and a half to four minutes each, with MC linking lines and pre-recorded VCRs in between.

About forty-five minutes into the live broadcast, the door was knocked.

Assistant PD Lee Sang-woo poked his head in to remind Baek Si-on to head to the interview cubicle for the number-one-contender vote-pitching segment.

The moment Baek Si-on sat up from the couch, stylist Park Ji-hun met him on the spot.

Left hand pressing his chin to hold the angle, right hand pinching an oil-absorbing sheet and pressing down precisely twice — once on each side of the nose, then once on the centre of the forehead — then tossing the sheet away and pulling a dry-brush from his waist bag, dipping it in the finest transparent oil-control powder, and sweeping it quickly over the T-zone.

The whole thing in under two minutes.

"Done."

Baek Eun-ah watched Park Ji-hun's work and mentally bumped his score up another point.

Ten out of ten.

Not afraid he'd get proud anymore.

✿ ✿ ✿

August 1 Live · interview segment KBS · the number-one interview cubicle Three camera angles, and a microphone on a stand

The interview was held in an extremely cramped little cubicle behind the stage.

Call it an interview room — it was really just a space walled off with mobile partitions, holding one camera, two fill lights, and a broadcast microphone.

The four members of SISTAR were already standing inside.

Park Seo-joon stood at the front, holding a cue card.

Baek Si-on walked in, and the space got even more crowded.

The two sides exchanged a brief nod.

The countdown beep sounded.

The red light above the camera snapped to solid.

Park Seo-joon's bright smile went up instantly.

"Ladies and gentlemen! This week's Music Bank number-one contenders: the source queen who's swept the summer again with 'Touch My Body' — SISTAR!"

The four of SISTAR waved in unison, beaming.

Park Seo-joon turned to Baek Si-on.

"And, with the digital single 'Way Back Home' making a stunning run up Melon — Baek Si-on!"

Baek Si-on waved toward the lens.

Bora picked up the next line, her tone sweet just to the right degree:

"The competition between our two contender groups is fierce! First, SISTAR — if you take number one today, what promise do you have for your fans?"

Park Seo-joon handed the mic to Hyolyn.

Hyolyn took it, cleared her throat, and said:

"If SISTAR gets number one today—"

She paused to build the suspense.

"We'll perform a gorilla dance on the encore stage, wearing diving goggles!"

She broke first, snorting with laughter, then beat her chest with both fists like a gorilla.

Beside her, Soyou and Dasom fell in with "ooo ooo" ape calls.

Bora did one too.

The cubicle dissolved into laughter.

"Whoa! Something to look forward to!"

Park Seo-joon led the applause, then turned his gaze to Baek Si-on.

"And Baek Si-on-ssi? This song's made a miracle run up Melon recently — if you take number one today, anything you'd like to promise your fans?"

Baek Si-on took the mic Bora handed over.

The three members of SISTAR turned their heads in unison.

Going on their first impression of Baek Si-on, they figured this sunbae might say something like "take number one and perform one-handed handstand singing," or "crush an apple barehanded live on stage"...

August 1 Live · downstairs KBS · the shared floor The common rest space for every group without a room

This was the common rest space for all the music-show groups that hadn't earned the right to a private waiting room that day.

Dozens of young men and women in garish stage outfits sat cross-legged on foam mats, or squeezed into a few wobbling plastic folding chairs.

The wall-mounted TV was playing the number-one-contender interview live.

Baek Si-on's cold, sharp face filled the whole screen.

"If I'm fortunate enough to take number one, I'll donate the entire week's streaming revenue across all platforms from 'Way Back Home' to the Korea Anti-Domestic-Violence Aid Foundation."

The already-quiet resting floor got quieter.

Everyone held the position they'd been in without moving.

It wasn't the rules that made them quiet.

It was the words.

One was a joke of a promise. The other was a donation of earnings.

Put those two together on the same broadcast and the contrast was so enormous it looked like two different channels' programming had been spliced together by mistake.

Someone raised a hand first, and slapped it down hard.

Then a second. Then a third.

The applause spread like a lit fuse, racing through the tight, crowded hall until it knitted into one dense wave of sound.

A few newly debuted boy-group idols stared at the screen, and a completely unprepared exclamation slipped out:

"Aish, how cool is this guy?!"

At the edge of the applause.

Red Velvet sat in a row of four.

Park Soo-young leaned slightly toward Irene.

"Eonni, do you think Baek sunbae can take number one?"

"I don't know."

Irene didn't turn her head. Her gaze stayed on the buzz-cut man on the big screen.

"But I hope he does."
