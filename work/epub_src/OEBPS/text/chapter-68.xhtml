Chapter Sixty-Eight

The Shrimp-Crackers Diplomacy and the Strawberry-Milk Incident

August 1 After the encore KBS · the corridor backstage A line of juniors, a snack, and a swallowed piece of bread

The last note of the encore accompaniment still hung in the hall.

Baek Si-on carried the trophy in one hand and walked down the side steps off the stage.

At this moment, both sides of the corridor were packed with people pressed against the walls.

All of today's newly debuted groups and small-agency idols, arranged in two lines in order of arrival.

Hands flat against the seams of their trousers, standing perfectly straight.

The moment Baek Si-on's leather shoes set down on the first strip of non-slip matting in the corridor.

The nearest few young men snapped their heels together, upper bodies leaning forward in a textbook ninety-degree bow:

"Congratulations on taking number one!"

That shout was like pushing over the first domino.

With every step Baek Si-on took forward, the human walls on either side folded down in a wave, a swell of bowed backs rolling on down the length of the hall like a ripple.

The voices of dozens of people gathered into one, bouncing around the narrow, sealed passage, so loud the fluorescent fixtures overhead shook slightly.

Baek Si-on didn't wave, didn't deliver any sunbae lecture.

Just nodded his way gently through the unbroken stream of greetings.

Back in the single waiting room.

Baek Si-on set the trophy down on the makeup counter at his leisure.

"Let's go."

Baek Eun-ah was chatting with Choi Jin-ri on her phone and looked up at the words.

"Going now? We haven't gone to say goodbye to the head PD yet…"

"Ah, right."

Before Baek Si-on could turn, the door was knocked.

"Knock, knock—"

The door was pushed open from outside.

The head PD strode in, the smile lines at the corners of his eyes creased deep.

The astonishing ratings figure from the control room earlier had clearly already turned Baek Si-on, in his eyes, into a walking ratings ATM.

"Si-on-ssi!"

He stepped in one stride, and reached out both hands to take Baek Si-on's.

"Incredible today!"

Baek Si-on shook hands with him a couple times.

"It's your direction that's good. The lighting and camera placement were all very considered."

The head PD's smile grew even wider.

Being praised by an artist on directing technique was ten times more gratifying than being praised for the show itself.

"Ah, you're too kind. Come have dinner with us in a bit? There's a good barbecue place out back — the show's treating."

"Terribly sorry, PD."

Baek Si-on pointed at the clock on the wall.

"I told my mother before I left the house. She's already made dinner at home waiting for me."

The head PD blinked.

In front of a golden-tier chance to build a relationship with a man holding real power at the station, someone had actually used "going home for dinner" as the excuse.

But he quickly came down the stairs himself.

"Is that so — a real filial son. So next weekend's music-show stage — Si-on-ssi will be back, won't you?"

Baek Si-on smiled.

"I won't take up your stage. A good slot should be saved for the juniors who need to promote."

The head PD read the real meaning behind that polite refusal, didn't press, traded a couple of pleasantries, and turned to leave.

Baek Si-on glanced back at the makeup counter.

"All packed?"

Park Ji-hun had already returned every tool to its place, the makeup case latched shut.

Baek Eun-ah slung the crossbody bag across her chest, tucked her phone into her pocket, and picked up the trophy from the counter.

"Done."

"Let's go."

August 1 After the encore KBS · the corridor, by the shared-floor doorway Thirty-odd bodies standing at attention in three seconds

A few executive managers had been standing sentry in the hall the whole time.

Their job wasn't to guard — it was to keep watch.

Watch for any movement at the senior waiting-room doors, so they could get their own kids lined up to greet in advance.

When the handle of Baek Si-on's door moved.

The manager nearest to that end twisted his neck, pupils contracted, and the next second had shoved the shared-floor door open and dashed in.

"Quick, quick! Baek Si-on sunbae's coming out! Everyone out, get in line!"

The shared floor erupted.

Someone mid-bite on bread set it down, someone mid-drink capped the bottle, someone mid-touch-up tossed the puff, someone leaning against the wall with eyes closed pretending to rest jackknifed to his feet.

Thirty-odd people made the switch from "slumped" to "standing at attention" in under three seconds.

The manager, voice hushed at the door, yelled under his breath.

"Hurry up! Don't keep the sunbae waiting! Get out and stand!"

With a clatter, a crowd of people poured out the shared-floor door and lined the walls on either side of the corridor.

Hands flat against trouser seams.

Spines pulled straight.

Eyes fixed forward.

When Baek Si-on walked over from the other end of the corridor.

The two human walls against the walls had already fallen into perfect alignment.

With every step, the people on both sides bent down in unison.

"Thank you for your hard work!!!"

This time Baek Si-on didn't keep the flat, stern face.

There was a faint curve at the corners of his mouth, and as he passed each group, he gave a gentle nod.

After all, clocking out always made a person happy.

Especially after going hungry all day.

The human wall reached the middle stretch.

Red Velvet's four members stood on the right, pressed against the wall, all bowing together.

Baek Si-on nodded back the same way.

But as he passed Irene, a detail caught in the corner of his eye:

Her left cheek was bulged, lips pressed tightly, the line of her jaw distorted by whatever she'd stuffed in her mouth.

Plainly she'd been eating in the shared floor, heard the manager shout "the sunbae's coming out," and bolted without finishing chewing.

Couldn't swallow it. Couldn't spit it out.

So she just bowed with her cheeks puffed, praying the sunbae wouldn't look this way.

But Baek Si-on stopped.

Irene's body, mid-bow, froze.

"Bag."

Baek Eun-ah, reflexively, handed over the crossbody bag.

Baek Si-on's hand went in, rummaged around twice.

First drew out a bag of shrimp crackers, then a box of strawberry milk, and held them both out in front of Irene.

Irene's gaze first landed on the two things that had appeared out of nowhere.

Then her eyes travelled from that hand, along the wrist, along the cuff of the deep-grey blazer, all the way up.

Met Baek Si-on's utterly unruffled eyes.

"Too thin. Eat more."

The muscle in Irene's left cheek gave a sharp pang.

She mustered all her strength to swallow the mouthful of bread she hadn't chewed at all.

That one swallow went down too hard, choking her so her eyes rolled slightly, even a faint flush rising up the skin of her neck.

But still she reached out quickly with both hands and took the crackers and milk.

"Th… thank you, sunbae."

Baek Si-on nodded.

Didn't say another word.

Turned and kept walking forward.

Baek Eun-ah, makeup case in hand, walking behind, slowed half a beat as she passed Irene.

She blinked once at Irene.

"Work hard, eonni."

Then she set out with the swaggering arrogance of a ruler surveying her own territory, keeping close on Baek Si-on's heels, thoroughly savouring the supreme glory of a big sunbae.

✿ ✿ ✿

The sound of footsteps faded slowly.

Until Baek Si-on's back had entirely disappeared around the corner at the end of the corridor.

Only then did the low-pressure air that had been bearing down on everyone finally lift.

The dozens of newcomers packed in the corridor let out a long breath at once, taut spines collapsing in unison, and drifted back into the shared floor.

Waiting for the next big sunbae to clock out, or for whichever powerful PD to pass by, before surging out all over again to repeat the standing-at-attention and bowing.

As the crowd filtered back.

A member of a girl group that had debuted at the same time as Red Velvet edged over, gaze fixed straight on the box of strawberry milk in Irene's hand.

"Being pretty really does make a difference~"

"No… the sunbae just thought I was too thin, so…" Irene explained.

"Right, right, too thin, eat more~"

The girl with the sour face mimicked Baek Si-on's tone and repeated it.

"Why didn't he say that to me? I'm thin too, you know?"

She presented her profile to the group.

"You're just thin in the chest." A member of her same group cut in.

"…Shut up!"
