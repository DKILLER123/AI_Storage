 “Come in.”

 Si-on came out of his room and greeted him.

 “Samchon.”

 Jeong-hun looked up at him. The last time they’d met, the boy was getting his enlistment papers. The time before that, the funeral. In between, almost nothing. Not from lack of wanting — from fear. After his brother died, the sister-in-law had raised a child alone, and he’d thought he should help, but he’d never known how to begin. So he’d let it slip, year by year, until they were strangers.

 Walking through this door today wasn’t about bringing a script. It was six years of swallowed courage, finally stepped over the threshold.

 “Sit.”

 Chapter Fifty
 Formal and Casual
 
 

 
 July 24
 Night
 OSEN, the entertainment desk
 A man at the top of his game, by way of the bottom
 [CARD-END]
OSEN's entertainment division.

 Editor-in-chief Son Nam-won was lying back in his chair, killing time.

 The backrest was at its maximum recline. Both feet propped up on the computer tower under the desk, the phone balanced on his stomach, baseball scores scrolling.

 OSEN wasn't one of those traditional big papers with a large investigative team.

 Nor was it a paparazzi giant like Dispatch, where staff camped out for the stakeout and lived off exclusive bombshells.

 It was an online entertainment-and-sports news portal. Plainly: a media outlet that lived on agency press releases, anonymous insider tip-offs, and headlines its own editors had cooked up after racking their brains. Others dug for news with their feet. They did it with their mouths. Whatever got fed to them, they ate, then topped it with a breathless headline, slapped it on the front page, and the traffic came streaming in and the ad money with it.

 Today the whole Korean media circle was scrambling for the Baek Jeong-hun exclusive. Son Nam-won would love to have chased it. He just couldn't win. Four reporters under him: one writing a post-match baseball analysis, one staking out a variety show, and the other two on vacation.

 What was he supposed to fight with?

 So he chose to lie there and wait for the release. Something related to Baek Jeong-hun would come out eventually; when it did, he'd change the headline, copy-paste, and clock out.

 Just then, the phone rang.
